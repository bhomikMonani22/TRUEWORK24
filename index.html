Frequently asked

Questions, answered.

What happens after the 3-month free support ends? +

In Option A (buyout), you're free to handle maintenance yourself, hire anyone, or keep us on a retainer. In Option B, the ₹2L/year licence fee covers all maintenance, bug fixes, and updates.

Can I start with Option B and upgrade to full buyout later? +

Yes. Your ₹20L paid under Option B is credited against the ₹40L buyout price. You'd pay ₹20L at that point to own the software outright.

What if I need custom features later? +

New features are scoped separately and quoted by complexity. Small tweaks are included in the Option B retainer. Major new modules are quoted individually.

How are API costs handled? +

WhatsApp, video streaming, and SMS are billed directly to your accounts at provider rates. We never mark these up or take a cut.

Why is this cheaper than a Mumbai agency? +

We run lean by design — no office overhead, no account management layer, no sales team. That's why the same platform that costs ₹85L at an agency lands at ₹40L with us, without compromising on quality.
